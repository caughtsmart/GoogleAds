## Flags & recommendations (prioritised)

### 1. Brand Search broad match is drifting into the whole dice category

The negatives are working — no "warhammer" queries got through. But
"loaded dice" on BROAD match is now matching the generic dice market,
which the word "warhammer" doesn't catch. Yesterday's 79 clicks:

- **44 clicks (£6.24) genuine brand** — "loaded dice", "loaded dice
  barry", "loaded dice shop", "loaded dice ltd", "loaded dice uk".
- **35 clicks (£10.20, 62% of spend) generic** — "dice tray", "dnd dice",
  "weighted dnd dice", "necron dice", "skaven dice", "drukhari dice set",
  "kill team", "kill team starter", "dnd books", "blood rage board game".

**Recommendation: change the keyword from BROAD to PHRASE match.** Phrase
match on "loaded dice" keeps all 44 brand clicks (it matches any query
containing the phrase) and eliminates the entire generic category in one
change. The alternative — chasing it with negatives — is whack-a-mole
across every dice, DnD and Kill Team term in existence.

These are products Loaded Dice genuinely sells, so the point isn't that
the traffic is worthless — it's that PMAX already serves those queries at
5–7 ROAS, and paying £0.30/click for them from the brand-defence budget
is the expensive way to buy the same customer.

Expected effect: Brand Search spend drops to roughly £6–8/day, all of it
genuine brand defence.

### 2. Demand Gen awareness has become 3× more expensive — bidding is broken

Per the settled policy this campaign is exempt from conversion rules, so
this is flagged strictly on **awareness efficiency**, which is the metric
that matters for it. CPM has tripled since the YouTube actions went
secondary on 2 Aug:

| Date | Spend | Impressions | CPM |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 Aug | £32.05 | 13,880 | £2.31 |
| 3 Aug | £15.11 | 4,514 | £3.35 |
| 4 Aug | £20.03 | 2,453 | £8.16 |
| 5 Aug | £20.83 | 3,060 | £6.81 |

**Root cause:** the campaign still uses **Maximise Conversions** bidding,
but by design it now records zero conversions. The algorithm has no
signal to optimise toward, so it is bidding blind — spending the full £20
for a third of the reach it used to buy.

**Recommendation: switch bidding to Maximise Clicks** (with the £20/day
budget unchanged). This is the appropriate strategy for an awareness
objective and should restore reach per pound. Explicitly **not** a
suggestion to pause or cut it — it's about making the awareness budget
buy awareness efficiently. Action: `set_campaign_bidding_strategy`
id 24084301631, strategy `maximize_clicks`, with a CPC ceiling.
