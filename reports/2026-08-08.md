### ADDENDUM — investigated further; it is not a simple tag break

Graham supplied the Purchase goal detail: **"Google Shopping App
Purchase" = Needs attention**, **"Loaded Dice - GA4 (web) purchase" =
Active**. My first thought was to promote the healthy GA4 action to
Primary as a stopgap. **Checked, and that does not work** — the GA4
action shows the *same* collapse:

| Date | Shopping App value | GA4 purchase value |
|---|---|---|
| 2 Aug | £709 | £1,207 |
| 3 Aug | £630 | £653 |
| 4 Aug | £349 | £476 |
| 5 Aug | £137 | £181 |
| 6 Aug | £318 | £330 |
| 7 Aug | £33 | £33 |

Two independent conversion sources falling together rules out a single
broken tag. So I went to the site data, and found a second anomaly.

**Shopify sessions tripled on 4 Aug while conversion rate collapsed:**

| Date | Shopify sessions | Conversion rate |
|---|---|---|
| 2 Aug | 1,366 | 1.76% |
| 3 Aug | 1,346 | 1.78% |
| **4 Aug** | **4,097** | **0.63%** |
| 5 Aug | 1,914 | 0.84% |
| 6 Aug | 1,652 | 1.15% |
| 7 Aug | 1,679 | 0.66% |

The surge is **UK (~2×), US (~3×) and Singapore (392 sessions, absent
before)** — *not* Glopal's DE/NL/FR markets, which barely moved. That
profile (sudden foreign-market volume, near-zero conversion) reads like
bot or scraper traffic rather than customers.

**And GA4 moved the opposite way:** its sessions *halved* on 4 Aug
(9,745 → 4,280) while Shopify's tripled. Two analytics systems on the
same site disagreeing in direction means the measurement itself changed
on 4 Aug, independent of whatever the traffic did.

### Honest conclusion

**At least two things changed on 4 Aug, and I cannot cleanly separate
them from the data available:**

1. A large volume of low-quality traffic began arriving (Shopify-side).
2. Conversion measurement changed or degraded (Google Ads + GA4 side).

Either alone would depress reported ROAS; both together certainly do.
What is *not* in doubt is that **the business is fine** — 42-75 orders
and £1,588-2,766 a day throughout — so this is a measurement and traffic
problem, not a trading one. That is the part that matters for budget
decisions, and it is why every budget decision stays frozen.

### What to check (in priority order)

0. **What changed on 4 Aug?** This is the fastest route to the answer and
   Graham will know it instantly: a Shopify theme update, an app
   installed or removed, a cookie/consent banner change, a Cloudflare or
   bot-protection setting, or a change to the Glopal integration. Every
   symptom above starts on that exact date.

1. **Google Ads → Goals → Conversions → the "Purchase" goal → "Needs
   attention"**. Read the specific warning; it should name the problem
   (tag inactive, no recent conversions, misconfigured).
2. **Shopify → Google & YouTube app** — this feeds the "Google Shopping
   App Purchase" action, which is the account's only primary purchase
   conversion. Check the connection is healthy and conversion tracking is
   still enabled. A disconnect or re-auth here would produce exactly this
   pattern.
3. If neither is conclusive, compare against GA4 purchases for the same
   days to confirm which side of the pipe is broken.

I can't diagnose further from the API — conversion tag health isn't
exposed through Windsor.
