# Investigation — the word "Download" in a Loaded Dice listing

Graham flagged a Google listing reading **"Download Warhammer 40k 11th
Edition Starter Sets"** on `us.loadeddice.uk`, with the note "we don't
sell downloads".

## Finding: it is not one of our ads

It is an **organic search result**, and the word is being introduced by
the **Glopal US mirror** (`us.loadeddice.uk`) — not by Google Ads, and
not by anything in Shopify.

Three independent checks rule out the ad account:

1. **No ad text in the account contains the word.** All 344 text assets
   across every campaign (246 advertiser-written, 98 Google
   auto-created) were pulled and searched: **zero** matches for
   "download". The auto-created assets are the usual "Shop EUGY 3D
   Models" / "Warhammer & Hobby Shop" stock — nothing invented.
2. **No campaign points at that domain.** No ad asset has a
   `us.loadeddice.uk` final URL, and the account has **no US campaign at
   all** — the only geo-expansion campaigns are Glopal's DE, NL and FR
   ones.
3. **The layout is organic.** Favicon, site name, URL and the "About
   this result" ⋮ menu, with no *Sponsored* label. Paid results carry
   that label in place of the URL line.

The only "download" exposure anywhere in the ad account is two search
terms, both on Glopal campaigns, both a single impression at zero cost:
"kill team downloads" (DE Brand) and "airbrush sjablonen downloaden"
(NL Shopping). Not related, and not worth acting on.

## Where the word is actually coming from

The page is our blog article *New Warhammer 40k 11th Edition Starter
Sets: A Beginner's Guide* (Shopify article `1001577644418`). Its source
metadata is clean:

| Field | Value in Shopify |
|---|---|
| `global.title_tag` | `Warhammer 40k 11th Edition Starter Sets: Beginner Guide` |
| `global.description_tag` | `New to Warhammer 40k? Our beginner's guide to every 11th edition starter set, helping you pick the right box to build, paint and play.` |

The word "download" does not appear in the title tag, the meta
description, or anywhere in the article body — the body's calls to
action are all "Shop All 40k Starter Sets", "Buy the Starter Set" and
similar. No product in the catalogue has "download" in its title either.

Comparing our metadata against what was rendered:

- **Title:** `...Starter Sets: Beginner Guide` → `Download ...Starter
  Sets`. The suffix has been dropped and a verb we never wrote has been
  prepended.
- **Description:** the opening hook `New to Warhammer 40k? ` has been
  stripped, leaving the remainder verbatim.

Both tags were altered in the same way, on the same page, on the mirror
domain only. Since the source is clean, the rewrite happens downstream
of Shopify — in Glopal's localisation layer, which serves and rewrites
`us.loadeddice.uk`. The most likely mechanism is that its en-GB → en-US
pass classifies a guide page as downloadable content and swaps in a CTA
verb.

**Not fully confirmed:** whether Glopal rewrites the tag itself or
Google rewrites Glopal's version. Outbound access to `loadeddice.uk` is
blocked from this environment, so the live US page could not be fetched.
One command settles it:

```
curl -s https://us.loadeddice.uk/blogs/blog/warhammer-40k-11th-edition-starter-sets | grep -io '<title>[^<]*</title>'
```

If it returns "Download…", Glopal is rewriting the tag. If it returns
our real title, Google is rewriting the SERP — unusual, since Google
draws rewrites from on-page text and "download" appears nowhere on the
page.

## Context: the mirror is indexed and ranking

Search Console is a domain property (`sc-domain:loadeddice.uk`), so it
covers the mirror. Over 30 days this one article ranks on both:

| Host | Typical position | Clicks |
|---|---|---|
| `loadeddice.uk` | 7-9 | 19 |
| `us.loadeddice.uk` | 15-40 | 0 |

The mirror earns impressions but no clicks — consistent with a title
that misdescribes the page.

## Recommendations

1. **Nothing to change in Google Ads.** No ad copy anywhere says
   "download"; no campaign serves that domain.
2. **Run the curl check above** to confirm which layer is rewriting.
3. **If it is Glopal, raise it with them:** their localisation is
   rewriting our meta titles and inserting CTA verbs that misdescribe
   the product. Ask that source `title_tag` and `description_tag` be
   passed through unmodified. This is a brand-accuracy issue, and if it
   affects guide pages generally it will be on more than this one URL.
4. **Worth asking Glopal how many mirror pages are affected** — the
   rewrite pattern looks templated, not one-off.
