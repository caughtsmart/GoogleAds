## ✅ Brand Search impression share — resolved, no action

Yesterday I flagged Brand Search's 12% impression share as "the most
promising thread in the account". Having dug in, **it is a false alarm,
and I was reading the wrong metric.**

| Metric (14d) | Value |
|---|---|
| Search impression share | 22.9% |
| Lost to **budget** | **0.25%** |
| Lost to rank | 51.0% |
| **Exact-match impression share** | **81.0%** |
| Absolute top impression share | 39.9% |

The headline impression share is low because the *eligible pool* for a
broad-matched keyword includes the entire dice/40k/D&D category the
campaign deliberately does not chase. On queries that actually match the
brand keyword, it captures **81% of impressions** — close to saturation.

Budget is not the constraint (0.25% lost). There is no missing brand
traffic to recover. **No action, thread closed.**

*Lesson for future runs: for a brand campaign, judge coverage on
exact-match impression share, not the headline figure.*

**Recommendation: raise EUGY's daily budget £12.50 → £15.00 (+20%).**

15000000 micros. Small absolute risk (£2.50/day), and the constraint is

keep if ROAS holds above 3.5, revert if it drops below 3.0.

It does not clear the playbook's formal ROAS ≥ 5 scale-up bar, which was
written for PMAX-sized decisions. I think a £2.50/day test on a
demonstrably budget-capped campaign is a reasonable exception, but it is
your call.

**This also supports yesterday's PMAX decision.** PMAX loses 69.8% of
